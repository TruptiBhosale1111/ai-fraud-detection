✅ How to verify a genuine offer yourself

1
Search the company on LinkedIn and check if the recruiter's profile actually exists
2
Open the company's official careers page and confirm the vacancy is listed there
3
Check the email domain — real HR writes from @company.com, never from Gmail
4
For loans, verify the lender on the RBI NBFC register at rbi.org.in
5
Never pay any fee — registration, security, training or processing
6
Never share Aadhaar, PAN or bank details before a signed offer letter
